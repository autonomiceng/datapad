# Own invoice intent locally and recover each provider effect

Datapad owns immutable invoice requests, customer ownership, lines, issue authorization and recovery obligations in PostgreSQL. Stripe owns issued invoice and payment state; local status is a projection with a retrieval time. The earlier sandbox proof established the manual standalone-invoice path, but its single-operator filesystem state does not provide durable application concurrency. Imported observations remain separate from operational billing, and invoice origin keys do not depend on a future recurring scheduler.

Persist first-attempt times and provider receipts on their customer, invoice and line records. Serialize effects with PostgreSQL session advisory locks, committing intent before external calls. pg-boss supplies at-least-once delivery; durable pending issuance and event receipts allow a bounded sweep to recover missed queue work. A generic operation ledger would add a second lifecycle model without a concrete need in this slice.

Recover lost responses by exact owned provider identity and immutable content. Finite provider idempotency retention means an old missing receipt cannot safely authorize recreation. Ambiguous outcomes stop for review, trading automatic progress for protection against duplicate invoices or lines. Signed events persist their retrieval obligation before acknowledgement, including events that arrive before a local provider receipt. Current provider retrieval and monotonic terminal states determine payment status. Recurrence, consent, automatic collection and notifications require later decisions.
